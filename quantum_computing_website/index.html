Course Description   This course introduces the fundamental concepts of Quantum Computing, including qubits, superposition, entanglement, quantum gates, quantum circuits, quantum algorithms, and quantum error correction. The course also explores practical implementations using Qiskit and quantum computing simulators.

Course Information

Prerequisites
Basic knowledge of linear algebra, complex numbers, probability, quantum mechanics, and Python programming.
